Revenue Reporting

Three reporting layers.
One source of truth.

We build reporting from the bottom up — contact-level funnel metrics inform deal metrics, deal metrics inform revenue metrics. Each layer connects to real HubSpot reports with defined formulas and benchmarks.

LAYER 01
Contact Funnel
How leads move through stages. Identifies where volume drops and where conversion is underperforming.
LEAD → MQL RATE
MQLs created ÷ Total leads
Benchmark: 15 – 25%
MQL → SQL RATE
SQLs created ÷ MQLs created
Benchmark: 30 – 50%
TIME TO MQL
Avg days: lead created → MQL date
Target: < 14 days
LEAD SOURCE QUALITY
MQL rate by hs_analytics_source
Top 3 sources by MQL volume
SCORE DISTRIBUTION
Contacts by score bucket (0-40, 40-70, 70+)
Monthly trend view
RECYCLED MQL RATE
MQLs reverted to lead ÷ total MQLs
Target: < 20%
LAYER 02
Pipeline & Velocity
How deals flow through the pipeline — speed, size, and stage conversion. The operating heartbeat of the sales org.
PIPELINE COVERAGE
Total pipeline value ÷ Revenue target
Target: 3× – 4× coverage
DEAL VELOCITY
# Opps × Win rate × ACV ÷ Sales cycle
Revenue per day measure
STAGE CONVERSION
Deals advancing ÷ Deals entering each stage
By stage, by AE, by segment
AVERAGE SALES CYCLE
Avg days: SQL created → Close date
By deal size bucket
WIN RATE
Closed-won ÷ (Won + Lost)
Benchmark: 20 – 30% for outbound
STALE DEAL RATE
Deals with no activity > 14 days
Triggers CS alert workflow
LAYER 03
Revenue & Retention
The board-level view — ARR, expansion, churn, and NRR. Built to be accurate, attributable, and auditable.
NEW ARR
Sum of ACV from new logos (period)
vs. quota by rep & team
EXPANSION ARR
Upsell + cross-sell on existing accounts
Target: >20% of new ARR
CHURNED ARR
ARR from customers lost (period)
Target: < 5% gross churn/year
NET REVENUE RETENTION
(ARR start + expansion - churn) ÷ ARR start
Best-in-class: > 120%
REVENUE ATTRIBUTION
First touch, last touch, U-shaped, W-shaped
Multi-touch per deal
FORECAST ACCURACY
Actual closed ÷ Forecast at period start
Target: ±10% variance
