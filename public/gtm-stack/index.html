Lead Scoring Dimensions
  • Behavioral (0–50 pts): page visits, email opens, clicks, content downloads, demo requests, pricing visits
  • Demographic (0–30 pts): job title seniority, company size, industry match, tech stack fit
  • Firmographic (0–20 pts): revenue range, funding stage, growth signals, tech adoption
  • Intent (0–30 bonus pts): Bombora surge score, G2 profile views, competitor site visits
  • Decay: -2pts/week of inactivity; score resets on major lifecycle change
Routing & Assignment Logic
  • Geographic routing: territory defined by country/state, assigned to regional SDR pod
  • Segment routing: SMB (<200 emp.) → SMB SDR; MM (200-2K) → Mid-Market SDR; ENT → named AE
  • Source-based routing: inbound demo → immediate AE; cold email reply → SDR qualify first
  • Round-robin cap: max 50 MQLs/SDR/week; overflow to next available
  • Escalation: if no SDR response in 4h → notify SDR manager
AI-Powered Operations
  • Email personalization: AI pulls CRM fields + LinkedIn bio to write first-line icebreakers at scale
  • Churn prediction: ML model trained on login frequency, support tickets, NPS, and feature adoption
  • Deal summarization: AI generates deal brief from all CRM activity for pipeline review meetings
  • Sequence optimization: A/B test subject lines; AI selects winner after 200 sends
  • Intent-triggered outreach: Bombora surge → auto-enroll target accounts in campaign within 24h

Attribution Models Used
  • First-touch: credit to channel that created the contact — good for awareness investment decisions
  • Last-touch: credit to channel directly before conversion — useful for evaluating bottom-of-funnel
  • U-shaped (40/20/40): 40% first, 40% last, 20% distributed — balanced for B2B
  • W-shaped: MQL creation gets additional weight — best for measuring SDR contribution
  • Custom: configurable per campaign type in HubSpot attribution reports
Forecasting Methodology
  • Bottom-up: sum of weighted pipeline by close date × stage probability
  • Historical run-rate: trailing 90-day close rate applied to current pipeline
  • Segment-adjusted: different close rates applied per segment (SMB vs. Enterprise)
  • Upside / Commit tiers: AE categorizes each deal as commit, best-case, or upside
  • AI overlay: regression model trained on historical close data adjusts probability in real time
KPIs Tracked Weekly
  • New ARR vs. quota (by rep, team, segment)
  • Net Revenue Retention (NRR) — expansion + churn
  • Pipeline coverage ratio (target: 3×–4× quota)
  • MQL→SQL rate, SQL→Opp rate, win rate
  • CAC by channel, blended CAC, CAC payback period
  • LTV:CAC ratio (target: >3×)
  • Average sales cycle length (trending by quarter)

AI Automation Layer

Where intelligence
meets execution.

The AI layer is not a bolt-on — it's the connective tissue between data signals and GTM actions. Here are four of the core AI systems we design and build for clients.

System 01

Predictive Lead Scoring

A multi-dimensional scoring model that combines behavioral signals, firmographic fit, intent data, and engagement recency into a single score that predicts conversion probability — not just activity volume.

Scoring Formula
score = (behavioral × 0.40) + (firmographic × 0.25) + (demographic × 0.20) + (intent_signal × 0.15) decay: score × 0.95 per 7 days inactive cap: max 100 pts, min 0 pts threshold: MQL at ≥ 40, high-priority at ≥ 70
MQL trigger SDR priority queue Sequence selection Personalization tier
System 02

Intent-Based Auto-Routing

When Bombora detects a company surging on topics like "CRM migration" or "revenue operations," the system automatically triggers a targeted outreach campaign within 24 hours — before the prospect even reaches out.

Routing Decision Tree
if intent_surge AND company in ICP: → enroll_in: intent_outreach_sequence → assign: territory_SDR (priority) → set: contact.intent_triggered = true → notify: "#intent-alerts" Slack if intent_surge AND no CRM record: → create: contact via enrichment API → route: as above
24h outreach trigger Territory assignment Slack alert Contact creation
System 03

Churn Risk Detection

A continuous monitoring system that cross-references product usage, support history, NPS, email engagement, and billing signals to produce a real-time health score for every customer account — with automated CS intervention.

Risk Score Composition
health_score = 100 − (days_since_login × 1.5) // max −30 − (open_tickets × 5) // max −25 − (nps_detractor × 20) // if NPS ≤ 6 + (feature_depth × 2) // breadth of use + (expansion_activity × 10) // upsell signals critical: < 30 → immediate CS task at_risk: 30–60 → check-in within 7 days healthy: > 60 → standard QBR cadence
Risk classification CS task auto-create Win-back sequence Leadership alert
System 04

AI-Powered Email Personalization

At scale, personalization dies because humans can't write 200 individual emails. We use LLMs (Groq / GPT-4) to pull live CRM data, recent company news, and the contact's LinkedIn headline to auto-generate personalized email openings for every sequence touchpoint.

Personalization Prompt Template
Given: contact.name = "{{first_name}}" contact.company = "{{company_name}}" contact.title = "{{jobtitle}}" enrichment.news = "{{recent_company_news}}" sequence.goal = "book discovery call" Write a 1-sentence email opener that: 1. References something specific about them 2. Does NOT start with "I" or "Congrats" 3. Feels human, not templated 4. Is under 20 words
Personalized opener Sequence inject A/B variant Send via HubSpot
